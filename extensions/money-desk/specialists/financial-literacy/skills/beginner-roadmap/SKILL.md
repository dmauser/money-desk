## Beginner Roadmap

description: 12-month sequenced personal-finance curriculum for someone starting from zero (or close to it). Each month has one focus, one thing to learn, one thing to do, and one diagnostic skill to run. The point: replace "where do I even start?" with "here's what you do this week", every week, for a year.

## When to use

- "I'm 22 and just started my first real job — where do I begin?"
- "I'm 35 and never really learned this stuff — give me a path."
- User is overwhelmed by Money Desk's 100+ skills and needs ONE thing to do this month.
- User wants a sequence to follow rather than a buffet.

## Inputs needed

- The user's high-level starting point. Just three datapoints:
  1. **Income source:** W-2 / 1099 / mixed / not yet earning.
  2. **Debt situation:** None / student loans only / credit-card debt / mortgage / mixed.
  3. **What's already set up:** Checking account / employer 401(k) enrolled / any retirement account / any brokerage.
- The roadmap below is the **default**; tweak based on those answers (e.g. skip month 4 "open a HYSA" if they already have one).

## Workflow

1. Collect the three datapoints above.
2. Pick the right starting month (most users start at month 1; some start at month 3 or later).
3. Walk through the 12-month plan, highlighting what to do THIS month and previewing next month.
4. After each month's task is done, the user returns and the next month's task gets activated.

## The 12-month curriculum

### Month 1 — Money diary
- **Focus:** awareness, not optimization.
- **Learn:** the difference between essential (rent, utilities, groceries, transport, insurance, min debt) and discretionary (everything else).
- **Do:** track every dollar for 30 days using any tool (Copilot Money, Monarch, YNAB, a spreadsheet — doesn't matter which).
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Cash Flow Audit** (Budget Coach, `md_bc_skill_cash_flow_audit`).
- **What you walk away with:** a one-page "where my money actually went last month" instead of an estimate.

### Month 2 — Concept literacy
- **Focus:** vocabulary.
- **Learn:** APR vs APY, gross vs net, pre-tax vs Roth, expense ratio, compound interest, tax bracket as a staircase.
- **Do:** read each definition; ask **Concept Explainer** at "beginner" depth for any term that doesn't click.
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Glossary Lookup** (Financial Literacy, `md_fl_skill_glossary_lookup`) — used freely throughout the month.
- **Walk away with:** the ability to read a pay stub and a brokerage statement without flinching.

### Month 3 — First emergency fund (mini)
- **Focus:** one month of essentials in a HYSA.
- **Learn:** why a HYSA exists; what FDIC insurance is and isn't.
- **Do:** open a high-yield savings account (Ally, Marcus, SoFi, etc.) and set up auto-transfer of $X / paycheck until you hit one month of essentials.
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Emergency Fund Assessment** (Net Worth & Goals, `md_nw_skill_emergency_fund_assessment`).
- **Walk away with:** breathing room; not the full 3-6 months yet, just one.

### Month 4 — 401(k) match
- **Focus:** capture every dollar of employer match.
- **Learn:** what a match is, how vesting works, traditional vs Roth 401(k).
- **Do:** log into your benefits portal, set your contribution % to AT LEAST the match threshold (most common: 4-6% of salary for full match). Choose a target-date fund if you can't pick — that's the right default.
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Contribution Optimizer** (Retirement Planner, `md_rp_skill_contribution_optimizer`) at light depth.
- **Walk away with:** an instant ~100% return on the matched contributions, every paycheck, forever.

### Month 5 — Debt triage
- **Focus:** map and prioritize all your debts.
- **Learn:** debt-avalanche (highest APR first) vs debt-snowball (smallest balance first). Math says avalanche; behavior often says snowball.
- **Do:** list every debt — creditor, balance, APR, minimum payment. Pick one method. Auto-pay the minimum on all; throw any extra at #1.
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Debt Payoff Strategy** (Debt Coach, `md_dc_skill_debt_payoff_strategy`).
- **Walk away with:** a written payoff order and an end date.

### Month 6 — Credit score basics
- **Focus:** what FICO actually rewards.
- **Learn:** payment history (35%), utilization (30%), age of credit (15%), mix (10%), new credit (10%). The two giant levers: on-time payment + < 10% utilization.
- **Do:** pull your free FICO from each card / bank that offers it; pull your full credit report (free yearly at annualcreditreport.com from each bureau). Dispute any errors. Set every recurring bill to auto-pay-minimum to lock in 100% payment history.
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Credit Score Diagnostic** (Credit Coach, `md_cr_skill_credit_score_diagnostic`).
- **Walk away with:** a score, a baseline, and any errors disputed.

### Month 7 — Insurance baseline
- **Focus:** the right amount, no more, no less.
- **Learn:** the four insurances most people genuinely need (health, auto if you drive, renters/homeowners, term life if dependents). What a deductible / premium / coverage limit is. Why whole life is usually wrong.
- **Do:** confirm you have appropriate health coverage; verify auto liability limits aren't state-minimum (raise to 100/300/100 if affordable); add renters insurance if you don't have it ($10-20/mo); evaluate term life only if anyone depends on your income.
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Coverage Audit** (Property & Casualty, `md_pc_skill_coverage_audit`) + **Life Insurance Needs** (Life & Disability, `md_lp_skill_life_insurance_needs`) if dependents.
- **Walk away with:** appropriate coverage on the things that could financially destroy you.

### Month 8 — Roth IRA
- **Focus:** the tax-advantaged account everyone should have if they can.
- **Learn:** Roth IRA basics; contribution limit ($7,000 for 2026 — verify with `md_facts`); income limits and the Backdoor Roth workaround for high earners.
- **Do:** open a Roth IRA at a low-cost custodian (Fidelity / Schwab / Vanguard). Set up auto-monthly transfers up to the annual limit. Invest in a 3-fund portfolio or single target-date fund.
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Contribution Optimizer** (Retirement Planner) — second pass with the Roth IRA layer added.
- **Walk away with:** a Roth IRA, funded automatically.

### Month 9 — Emergency fund finish
- **Focus:** complete the 3-6 month emergency fund.
- **Learn:** how much YOU need (single income vs dual; W-2 vs 1099; dependents; tenure) — not just "3 months" as a rule.
- **Do:** continue automated HYSA contributions until full target hit. Park in the same HYSA you opened in month 3.
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Emergency Fund Assessment** (Net Worth & Goals) — second pass, full sizing.
- **Walk away with:** the actual full emergency fund.

### Month 10 — Tax intuition
- **Focus:** know what affects your tax bill so you can plan, not panic.
- **Learn:** marginal vs effective rate; standard vs itemized deduction; how pre-tax 401(k) / HSA reduce your taxable income; what generates a refund (over-withholding) vs what generates a bill (under-withholding); estimated taxes if you have 1099 income.
- **Do:** run **Withholding Check** (Tax Planner) before October so you have time to adjust W-4 before year-end. Pull last year's 1040 and trace each line.
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Withholding Check** (Tax Planner, `md_tx_skill_withholding_check`).
- **Walk away with:** "I know roughly what I'll owe this year, and I won't be surprised."

### Month 11 — Investment basics
- **Focus:** understand what you own.
- **Learn:** stocks vs bonds; index funds vs active funds; expense ratio's compounding impact; asset allocation; rebalancing; what "diversification" actually means.
- **Do:** review your 401(k) / IRA holdings. Check expense ratios. If any holding has expense ratio > 0.50%, look for a low-cost alternative (target-date or index) in the same plan.
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Portfolio Review** (Investing Advisor, `md_iv_skill_portfolio_review`).
- **Walk away with:** a portfolio that won't slowly bleed you to death via fees.

### Month 12 — Net worth snapshot + next-year plan
- **Focus:** measure progress, set next year's targets.
- **Learn:** assets - liabilities = net worth; why the trend matters more than the absolute number; the three numbers worth tracking quarterly (net worth, savings rate, debt balance).
- **Do:** snapshot today; commit to quarterly check-ins; pick 2-3 specific goals for next year (e.g. "max Roth IRA", "kill the credit card", "open an HSA when I switch to HDHP").
- **Diagnostic skill:** **Net Worth Snapshot** (Net Worth & Goals, `md_nw_skill_net_worth_snapshot`).
- **Walk away with:** a number to beat next year, and a 12-month plan.

## Year 2+ — graduation

Once month 12 is done, the user is past "beginner". From here, the playbooks are situational:
- Buying a home → **Rent vs Buy** (Purchase Advisor) + **Buy vs Lease** for car decisions.
- Kids on the way → **Education / 529** + **Life Insurance Needs** uplift.
- Equity comp at work → **Investing Advisor** / **Tax Planner** for RSU / ISO / NQSO strategy.
- Real estate investing curious → **Rental Property Analyzer** (Real Estate Investor).
- Self-employment / side-business → **Self-Employed**.
- Moving abroad / cross-border → **International / Expat**.
- Estate planning prompt (kids, marriage, inheritance) → **Estate Planner**.

## Disclaimer

This is a default sequence designed for a typical W-2 earner with moderate debt. Aggressive customization is the diagnostic specialists' job — Roadmap just makes sure you're not lost.
